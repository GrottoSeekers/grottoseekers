01

The mark

A paw print with a keyhole cut through the pad and a globe held inside it. Three ideas in one silhouette: the animal, the front door, and the fact that the door could be anywhere in the world. Single colour always — never two-tone, never outlined.

What the parts mean

The paw. The pet is the reason the trip works at all. Four toes rotated outward, so the arc reads as a print rather than four dots.

The keyhole. A true knock-out through the pad — whatever is behind shows through, so the mark never needs redrawing for a new background.

The globe. Held in the bow of the keyhole. It is the only fine detail in the mark and the first thing to close up small — which is what sets the 32px floor.

Posting rules

One mark per frame. Top-left on photos, or centred on the story frame. Never both.

Real photos only. Sitter and owner photography, shot in daylight, no stock.

Caption as you’d text. Two lines, one detail, one link. Three hashtags maximum.

Type stays in Marcellus. Never set post copy in the wordmark tracking.
