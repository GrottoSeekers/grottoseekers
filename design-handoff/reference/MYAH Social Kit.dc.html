How to use these

Edit the copy in place. Click any line and type. Keep headlines to two lines and captions to one.

Swap photos, not crops. Drop a new image on any photo — it fills the same frame, so the layout holds.

One mark per frame. White over photos, cream on forest and ink, bark on paper and cream.

Exporting. Frames are built at true ratio — screenshot any one at 2× for a print-clean 2160px file.
