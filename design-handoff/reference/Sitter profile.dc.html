Who we are

A home away from home — for you and your pets.

We're Callum and Niamh. We've cared for all sorts of animals. From cats and dogs to horses and bearded lizards. We've stayed in just as many kinds of home. From high-rise city apartments to farmhouses on a hundred acres.

We treat every sit like it's our own home: daily walks, feeding routines kept to the minute, plants watered, bins out, and regular photo updates so you can relax while you're away.
